# SourceHut: ToS changes and LLMs

- URL: https://sourcehut.org/blog/2026-08-27-tos-changes-and-llms/
- Fetched: 2026-10-21 (WebFetch; model-extracted summary)

- Prohibited content now includes "original content written with or which facilitates the use of LLMs (large language models) or other generative AI technologies".
- LLM-produced code, assets, tickets and emails "will no longer be permitted on SourceHut once these terms take effect"; software with generative AI features "not welcome".
- Mirroring large codebases with looser policies allowed; private LLM use for review "discouraged but not prohibited".
- Light-touch enforcement; projects intending to keep using AI "are required to migrate away". Applies to new projects from September 10th (2026).
- Reasons: resource costs (build minutes, frequent commits, crawlers), climate, licensing, labour, maintainer burnout.
- Context (search): Codeberg announced its own LLM limits in late July 2026; Forgejo's March 2026 monthly report says the project prohibits AI-generated contributions (https://forgejo.org/2026-03-monthly-report/).
